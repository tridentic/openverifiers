# High School Mathematics - OpenVerifiers

## Supported domains

### 1. Algebra & Number Systems

- **Sets and Functions**
  - Set theory (operations, Venn diagrams)
  - Relations and functions
  - Domain, range, and function types
  - Graphs of functions

- **Real Numbers & Inequalities**
  - Properties of real numbers
  - Absolute value
  - Linear and quadratic inequalities
  - Systems of inequalities

- **Complex Numbers**
  - Complex number algebra
  - Argand diagram
  - Polar form of complex numbers
  - De Moivre's theorem
  - Roots of complex numbers

- **Polynomials & Polynomial Equations**
  - Polynomial algebra
  - Remainder and factor theorems
  - Roots of polynomial equations
  - Relationship between roots and coefficients
  - Solving polynomial equations

- **Binomial Expansion**
  - Binomial theorem for positive integral index
  - Binomial coefficients and properties
  - Applications of binomial theorem

### 2. Linear Algebra

- **Matrices**
  - Matrix types and operations
  - Transpose and symmetric matrices
  - Determinants (properties and evaluation)
  - Adjoint and inverse of a matrix
  - System of linear equations (Cramer's rule)
  - Matrix method for solving equations

### 3. Vectors & Geometry

- **Vectors**
  - Vector algebra (addition, subtraction, scalar multiplication)
  - Position vectors
  - Dot product and cross product
  - Scalar triple product and vector triple product
  - Applications of vectors in geometry

- **Straight Lines (Coordinate Geometry)**
  - Equations of straight lines (various forms)
  - Angle between two lines
  - Distance from a point to a line
  - Intersection of lines
  - Concurrent lines
  - Family of lines

- **Circles (Coordinate Geometry)**
  - Equation of a circle (standard and general forms)
  - Tangents and normals
  - Length of tangent
  - Common tangents to two circles
  - Orthogonal circles

- **Conic Sections**
  - Parabola (equation, properties, tangents, normals)
  - Ellipse (equation, properties, tangents, normals)
  - Hyperbola (equation, properties, tangents, normals)
  - Parametric equations of conics

### 4. Trigonometry

- **Trigonometric Ratios & Identities**
  - Trigonometric ratios of standard angles
  - Trigonometric identities
  - Compound angle formulas
  - Multiple and sub-multiple angle formulas
  - Transformation formulas (sum to product, product to sum)

- **Trigonometric Equations**
  - General solutions of trigonometric equations
  - Principal solutions

- **Inverse Trigonometric Functions**
  - Domain and range
  - Properties and identities
  - Solving equations involving inverse trig functions

- **Properties of Triangles**
  - Sine rule, cosine rule, tangent rule
  - Projection formula
  - Area of triangles
  - Circles related to triangles (incircle, circumcircle, excircles)
  - Heights and distances

### 5. Calculus

- **Functions & Limits**
  - Types of functions
  - Concept of limits
  - Evaluation of limits
  - Continuity of functions

- **Differentiation**
  - First principles
  - Rules of differentiation
  - Differentiation of implicit functions
  - Parametric differentiation
  - Higher-order derivatives
  - Logarithmic differentiation

- **Applications of Differentiation**
  - Tangents and normals
  - Rate of change
  - Increasing and decreasing functions
  - Maxima and minima
  - Optimization problems
  - Curve sketching

- **Integration**
  - Indefinite integrals
  - Standard integrals
  - Integration by substitution
  - Integration by parts
  - Integration using partial fractions
  - Definite integrals
  - Properties of definite integrals

- **Applications of Integration**
  - Area under curves
  - Area between curves
  - Volume of revolution (basic concepts)

- **Differential Equations**
  - Order and degree
  - Formation of differential equations
  - Solution of first-order differential equations
  - Applications of differential equations

### 6. Combinatorics & Probability

- **Permutations & Combinations**
  - Fundamental principle of counting
  - Factorial notation
  - Permutations (with and without repetition)
  - Combinations
  - Properties of nCr
  - Applications

- **Probability**
  - Basic probability concepts
  - Addition and multiplication theorems
  - Conditional probability
  - Independent events
  - Bayes' theorem (basic)

### 7. Statistics

- **Measures of Dispersion**
  - Range and mean deviation
  - Variance and standard deviation
  - Coefficient of variation
  - Quartiles and interquartile range

- **Data Analysis**
  - Frequency distributions
  - Graphical representation (histograms, frequency polygons)

### 8. Mechanics (Applied Mathematics)

- **Statics**
  - Forces and their resolution
  - Resultant of forces
  - Equilibrium of forces
  - Lami's theorem
  - Moments and couples
  - Friction (laws and applications)
  - Center of gravity

- **Dynamics (Motion of a Particle in a Plane)**
  - Rectilinear motion
  - Motion under gravity
  - Projectile motion
  - Relative velocity
  - Newton's laws of motion
  - Impulse and momentum
  - Work, energy, and power
  - Simple harmonic motion

### 9. Linear Programming

- **Linear Inequalities in Two Variables**
  - Graphical solutions
  - Feasible regions

- **Linear Programming Problems**
  - Formulation of LPP
  - Graphical method of solution
  - Optimization (maximization and minimization)
  - Applications in real-world problems
