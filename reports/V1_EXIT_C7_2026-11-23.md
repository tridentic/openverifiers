### D-030 spend (trusted code)

**1. Surd-valued uniqueness certificates.**
- **What was added:**
  - √k is an indeterminate of the normal form, reduced by (√k)² = k.
  - Positive-sum multipliers: terms c·h₁⋯h_k, optionally times q². c is signed
    > 0 by the checker, each h is a stated `>`/`<` hypothesis, and at least one
    term has no square.
- **Result:** **5 of the 5 JEE answers whose uniqueness rested on z3 now reach
  `{kernel}`**: phys p2q9, phys p2q3, phys p2q12, chem p2q12 and math p2q12
  (CTO decision 3).
- **The untrusted finder** gained a Gauss pre-pass, triangularization of the
  ideal pool, a conjugate step and positive divisors.

**2. π in integrands.** The trusted integrator combines Q(π) samples with the
rational weights of unit samples.
- **Three domains, all at `{kernel}`:** a disc's area, a ball's mass, the
  amount of solute in a tube.
- Before the change all three were UNKNOWN.

**3. ln in the exp ring.**
- **The rules:**
  - d ln u = u′·inv(u), and d inv(u) = −u′·inv(u)².
  - a / u is a·inv(u).
  - Every ln argument and every non-constant divisor needs a checked witness
    u = c·h₁⋯h_k·exp(…) > 0.
  - Only with a witness may a certificate use u·inv(u) = 1.
- **Three domains, all at `{kernel}`:** math p2q9's ODE closed form,
  first-order decay d ln a/dt = −k, and isothermal work dW/dV = nRT/V.
- **A gap caught before commit:** "d ln x = 1/x" cancels to 0 in the normal
  form, so a check over the cancelled goal accepted it with no witness. The
  check now reads the goal as written.

**The trusted-check rule (D-030):** no trusted check was removed or weakened in
C7. The OVState loader's sortedness and groundness checks, removed in C6 to fit
the old cap, are back, with tests.
