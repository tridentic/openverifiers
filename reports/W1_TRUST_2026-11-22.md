## 1. What was done (against W1 and MP-DOL)

- **Meaning gate (MP-DOL, D-023), `791b500`.**
  - Adding, changing or removing an axiom, law, definition, class invariant
    or main theorem statement in `packages/` or `laws/` now needs
    `Meaning-Approved-By: Arjo`. Continuation lines are included, and one
    trailer covers a commit.
  - Lemmas, proofs, tests and scenarios stay free.
  - The CI job `gold-gate` runs it.
  - Tests show an unapproved change rejected, new statements and
    continuation lines caught, lemmas and tests ignored, and only "Arjo"
    approving.
- **Exact-arithmetic audited unit (D-021), cap 450.** It holds
  `raw/surd.py`, `raw/interval.py`, `raw/numeric.py` and the new
  `raw/certificate.py`. `scripts/tcb_size.py` enforces all three caps, and
  the 2,000 total.
- **Certificate checker (trusted, `raw/certificate.py`).** It checks by exact
  polynomial normal form, with polynomial integrals and derivatives, that
  multiplier · (lhs − rhs) − Σ cᵢ (Aᵢ − Bᵢ) − Σ w q² is 0.
  - The multiplier is a product of hypotheses proven nonzero (positive, for
    inequalities).
  - This covers identities, ideal membership, and laws solved for one
    variable, whose side condition is the recorded factor.
- **Finder (untrusted, `objects/certify.py`, sympy).**
  - It tries multipliers from the hypotheses, reduces in several variable
    orders, and finds squares for ≥ goals.
  - Earlier certified theorems are used as lemmas.
  - Whatever it proposes is re-checked by the trusted unit.
  - A certified theorem is PROVEN with `trust {kernel}` and survives
    `--strict`.
- **Differential testing (`bench/differential.py`).**
  - Random expressions (arithmetic, sqrt, exp, ln, pi, polynomial calculus)
    are evaluated two ways: by the solver-side expander, with its atoms
    enclosed by intervals, and by the trusted interval evaluator. A disjoint
    pair is a disagreement.
  - It runs in CI (500 cases) and fails on any disagreement. A planted
    expander bug is caught.

## 5. Deviations

1. **Lemmas are not meaning-carrying.** The directive lets me write lemmas
   freely and gates "main theorem statements". So `theorem` lines are gated
   and `lemma` lines are not.
2. **No strict-inequality certificates.** The certificate format proves `=`
   and `≥`/`≤` only. Adding strict inequalities (a square of a nonzero factor
   plus a positive part) costs trusted lines; there are 37 left in the total
   budget.
3. **Certificates are tried after z3.** z3 still runs first. A certificate
   only upgrades trust, and a conflict with a confirmed counterexample is
   reported as a bug, never resolved silently.

## 6. Shortcuts found (searched deliberately)

1. **The exp chain rule has no trusted oracle.**
   - The differential test cannot check `deriv` of exp bodies: the trusted
     evaluator only differentiates polynomials.
   - So the decay rate-law theorem rests on the solver-side chain rule, with
     trust `z3_unsat`, and that rule is tested only by unit tests.
2. **Non-deterministic symbol names in the finder.** They used Python's salted
   hash, which could change the certificate search between runs. Fixed in
   this report's commit with a stable hash, and checked under two hash seeds.
3. **The meaning gate's continuation rule is a heuristic.**
   - A changed line belongs to the nearest statement head above it.
   - An odd layout could misattribute a line: a comment between a head and
     its continuation is treated as ending the statement.
   - Covered by tests for the normal layouts only.
4. **Broad exceptions in bench tooling.** `except Exception` appears in
   `bench/run.py` and `bench/reductions.py` (untrusted). A crash is recorded,
   never matched to gold.
5. **`type: ignore` in untrusted code.** There are five, in `expand.py` and
   `certify.py`. They don't touch trusted code.
