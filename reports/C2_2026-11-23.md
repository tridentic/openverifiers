**Q(π) (D-026.1).**
- `raw/qpi.py` (trusted, unit A) represents a value as p(π)/q(π) over Q:
  - equality is decided by exact normal form, since π is transcendental;
  - sign and order come from a rigorous interval enclosure of π, and are
    refused when the enclosure can't decide.
- The evaluator now computes `pi` exactly in Q(π).
- z3's models put a rational stand-in for π. An untrusted search
  (`objects/qpi_lift.py`) re-solves the π-dependent variables symbolically,
  and the trusted checker re-checks the result exactly.
- **Effect:**
  - phys p1q13 (de Broglie / Bohr) is solved: α = 72, model h = 2π/3 checked
    exactly;
  - the circle circumference is now exactly 2π, where it used to be an
    interval;
  - the solid sphere's mass (4/3 πρR³) is checked exactly.

   It includes the 58 earlier `packages/` statements that were never
   approved, because they would otherwise sit outside the founder's approval

## 6. Shortcuts found

1. **The uniqueness-certificate search is heuristic.** Pseudo-division over
   derived ideal members needs every leading coefficient to factor into
   stated nonzero hypotheses. The 4 misses above are all of that kind. The
   checker is unaffected: only the search misses.
2. **`pi > 0` is passed to the certificate checker as a hypothesis.** It is a
   mathematical fact, but it enters the same way class invariants do, as a
   caller-supplied hypothesis. The checker's contract says the caller passes
   only true hypotheses. Recorded here so it is not a silent trust addition.
3. **The Q(π) lift picks the variables to re-solve by the size of their
   numerators and denominators** (> 10⁸). That is a heuristic. A wrong pick
   only fails the trusted re-check, giving UNKNOWN, never a wrong PROVEN.
4. **Test changes that followed results:**
   - The circle circumference test now expects exact 2π (was an interval).
   - The tests of the removed interval `sin`/`cos` were removed.
   - A gold-gate test now rejects a non-listed approver (C1).
