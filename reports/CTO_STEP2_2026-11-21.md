## 4. Deviations: please confirm or overrule

1. **The integer-search budget is 10,000 nodes, not 200 ms.**
   - With a wall-clock budget, the false-Rhombus test gave REFUTED here and
     UNKNOWN in CI: the slower runner did not reach the counterexample
     (3,651 nodes, 55 ms here). That turned 3 of 4 test jobs red.
   - A node count gives the same verdict on every machine. 10,000 nodes is
     about 200 ms here. A 2 s wall clock remains only as a backstop.
   - Cost: geometry takes 3.66 s, against 3.54 s with the clock.
2. **Theorem checks fall back to the integer search** only when z3 neither
   proves nor refutes. It never runs on a proof that succeeds. Without the
   fallback, `ov check` leaves the false Rhombus theorem UNKNOWN.
3. **`ov check` = the full pipeline with one-line output.** The 300 ms check
   covers rule scenarios only. `square.ov` (object layer) takes 0.95 s and is
   not held to it.
