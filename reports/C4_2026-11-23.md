### Uniqueness certificates (CTO decision 3)

- Signs now come from the checker: closed constants such as π are signed by
  the checker's own enclosure (C3), and weighted squares were added.
- Of the 5 answers whose uniqueness rested on z3 at C2, **1 reaches
  `{kernel}`** (phys p2q9). The other 4 are unchanged:

  | answer | why it still rests on z3 |
  |---|---|
  | chem p2q12 | the multiplier rate ≠ 0 is a derived quantity, and the finder finds no product of stated hypotheses for it |
  | math p2q12 | needs Q(α, β) ≠ 0, which follows from α ≠ β; no factor of a stated hypothesis gives it |
  | phys p2q3 | the value is a surd |
  | phys p2q12 | needs a positive square root |

- **Surd-valued certificates (Q(√d)) are not done.** They need √d as an
  indeterminate with the reduction (√d)² = d, plus a checker-verified sign
  for the multiplier r + √d. My estimate is above the 15 trusted lines the
  decision allows, so I did not start.
- **Checker-verified nonzero multipliers** (enclosures of derived quantities;
  factors of stated nonzero hypotheses) are not done either. Neither shape
  occurs in the 4 remaining answers, so it would move none of them.
