# High School Physics - OpenVerifiers

## Supported domains

### 1. Foundations & Measurement

- Physical world and measurement
- Units, dimensions, and SI system
- Error analysis and uncertainty
- Scalars and vectors (vector algebra, resolution, dot/cross products)

### 2. Kinematics & Dynamics

- Motion in one and two dimensions
- Equations of motion (uniform acceleration)
- Projectile motion
- Newton's laws of motion
- Inertia, momentum, impulse
- Friction and its applications
- Circular motion (centripetal force, banking of roads)

### 3. Work, Energy & Power

- Work-energy theorem
- Conservation of energy
- Power and efficiency
- Conservative and non-conservative forces

### 4. Gravitation & Celestial Mechanics

- Newton's law of universal gravitation
- Gravitational field and potential
- Variation of g with altitude, depth, and latitude
- Satellite motion and orbital velocity
- Escape velocity
- Weightlessness

### 5. Properties of Matter

- Elasticity (stress, strain, Hooke's law, Young's modulus)
- Surface tension and capillarity
- Viscosity and Stokes' law
- Bernoulli's principle and applications
- Fluid flow (laminar vs. turbulent)

### 6. Periodic Motion & Waves

- Simple harmonic motion (SHM)
- Pendulums (simple and compound)
- Wave types and characteristics
- Superposition of waves
- Standing waves and resonance
- Sound waves and Doppler effect
- Beats and acoustics

### 7. Thermodynamics & Kinetic Theory

- Temperature and heat
- Thermal expansion (solids, liquids, gases)
- Specific heat capacity and calorimetry
- Phase changes and latent heat
- Heat transfer (conduction, convection, radiation)
- Ideal gas laws and kinetic theory
- Thermodynamic processes (isothermal, adiabatic, isobaric, isochoric)
- First and second laws of thermodynamics
- Carnot engine and entropy
- Heat engines and refrigerators

### 8. Electrostatics

- Electric charge and Coulomb's law
- Electric field and field lines
- Electric flux and Gauss's law
- Electric potential and potential difference
- Capacitors and capacitance
- Energy stored in capacitors
- Dielectrics

### 9. Current Electricity

- Electric current and drift velocity
- Ohm's law and resistance
- Resistivity and conductivity
- Series and parallel combinations
- Kirchhoff's laws
- Wheatstone bridge and meter bridge
- Potentiometer
- Heating effect of current (Joule's law)
- Electrical power and energy

### 10. Magnetism & Magnetic Effects of Current

- Magnetic field and field lines
- Biot-Savart law
- Ampere's circuital law
- Force on moving charge in magnetic field
- Force on current-carrying conductor
- Magnetic effects of current (solenoid, toroid)
- Earth's magnetism
- Magnetic properties of materials (diamagnetic, paramagnetic, ferromagnetic)
- Hysteresis

### 11. Electromagnetic Induction & AC

- Faraday's and Lenz's laws
- Self and mutual induction
- Inductance (self and mutual)
- AC generators and transformers
- Alternating current (AC) fundamentals
- RMS and peak values
- AC circuits (R, L, C, LCR series)
- Resonance in AC circuits
- Power in AC circuits

### 12. Geometrical Optics

- Reflection and mirrors (plane and spherical)
- Refraction and Snell's law
- Total internal reflection
- Lenses (thin lens formula, lens maker's formula)
- Combination of lenses
- Optical instruments (microscope, telescope)
- Dispersion and prisms
- Scattering of light

### 13. Physical Optics (Wave Optics)

- Huygens' principle
- Interference (Young's double slit)
- Diffraction (single slit, gratings)
- Polarization
- Coherence

### 14. Modern Physics

- Photoelectric effect and Einstein's equation
- Wave-particle duality
- de Broglie wavelength
- Heisenberg uncertainty principle
- Atomic models (Thomson, Rutherford, Bohr)
- Atomic spectra (hydrogen spectrum)
- X-rays (production, properties, Moseley's law)
- Lasers (basic principles)

### 15. Nuclear Physics

- Structure of nucleus
- Nuclear forces and binding energy
- Mass defect and mass-energy equivalence
- Radioactivity (alpha, beta, gamma decay)
- Radioactive decay law and half-life
- Nuclear fission and fusion
- Nuclear reactors
- Radiation detectors

### 16. Semiconductor Electronics

- Energy bands (conductors, insulators, semiconductors)
- Intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors
- p-n junction diode and characteristics
- Rectifiers (half-wave, full-wave)
- Transistors (BJT, configurations, amplification)
- Logic gates (AND, OR, NOT, NAND, NOR)
- Integrated circuits (basic concepts)

### 17. Relativity & Astrophysics

- Special theory of relativity (postulates, time dilation, length contraction)
- Mass-energy equivalence (E=mc²)
- Stars and stellar evolution
- Galaxies and the universe
- Hubble's law and expanding universe
- Black holes (basic concepts)
- Cosmology (Big Bang theory)
